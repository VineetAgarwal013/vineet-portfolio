# MBA

## Current status

Vineet is an MBA student at Rice University.

## Why the MBA

After four years at ZS, Vineet wanted to move from consulting toward building products.

The MBA is part of that career transition and gives him an opportunity to broaden his business knowledge while developing toward product management.

## What Vineet is learning

The MBA is exposing Vineet to areas such as finance, accounting, and other business disciplines that he had not previously worked with deeply.

He values the rigor of the program and the opportunity to learn from classmates with different backgrounds and perspectives.

## Learning from people

One of the things Vineet appreciates most about business school is being surrounded by a diverse group of people.

He has learned that he does not need to be the smartest person in the room about everything.

Being around people with different experiences gives him perspectives that he would not get from reading a case or report alone.

## Career direction

Vineet's long term direction is AI Product Management.

He wants to combine his experience in customer research, analytics, data science, and product building to create products that solve real customer problems.

## Why AI Product Management

Vineet is interested in AI products because they can be powerful tools for solving customer pain points and eliminating unnecessary work.

He is not interested in AI simply because a product uses AI. His starting point is the customer problem.

The approach is:

Start with the user.

Understand the problem.

Research the pain point.

Use data to understand it.

Build the right solution.
