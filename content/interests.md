# Interests

## Sports

Vineet enjoys playing almost every sport but is much more interested in participating than watching sports.

## Mixed Martial Arts

Mixed martial arts has been particularly meaningful to Vineet.

MMA taught him discipline, mental patience, emotional control, and physical strength.

He trained intensely, including waking up around 5 AM and spending several hours training.

He valued the community as much as the physical training.

The trust involved in training with people who help each other improve and push physical limits was an important part of the experience.

After moving to the United States, Vineet stopped training because he had not yet found a community that gave him the same sense of trust and belonging.

He would like to return to MMA when he finds the right community.
