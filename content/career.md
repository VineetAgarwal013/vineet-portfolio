# Career

## ZS Associates

Vineet spent approximately four years at ZS Associates, primarily working with healthcare clients and problems involving customers, decisions, analytics, data science, and products.

A consistent theme throughout his work was understanding customers and understanding how people make decisions. This was the part of the work that motivated him most.

## Year 1: Analytics

Vineet started in an analytics role.

His work involved numbers, Excel, quantitative methods, and statistics. This gave him a strong foundation in quantitative analysis and business problem solving.

## Year 2: Data Science

Vineet then worked with a data science team.

He built machine learning models to analyze data and create recommendation models for ZS's proprietary tool, Zeta.

This experience helped him connect quantitative analytics with machine learning and practical business recommendations.

## Year 3: Product Management

During his third year, Vineet began moving toward product management.

He became increasingly interested in not just analyzing problems, but deciding what should actually be built to solve them.

## High impact pricing work

One of Vineet's most significant professional projects involved pricing GLP 1 weight loss medications across multiple markets.

He helped develop pricing recommendations across seven countries. The client used the recommendations to support product pricing and scaling decisions.

The exact client cannot be disclosed.

The work had significant commercial impact. Vineet describes the broader pricing work as contributing approximately $15 million in cumulative annual revenue, with approximately $4 million to $5 million associated with one quarter.

These figures should be treated as Vineet's stated project impact and should not be expanded or embellished by the chatbot.

## Product building at ZS

In his final year at ZS, Vineet increasingly worked with internal ZS teams to build products for them.

He became obsessed with creating tools that removed mundane and repetitive processes.

He sometimes spent significant time building tools that could reduce roughly 30 percent to 40 percent of manual human work.

This led to more opportunities to build internal products and ultimately helped him discover that product management was the direction he wanted to pursue.

## Career transition

After four years at ZS, Vineet reached a decision point.

He could continue progressing as a consultant, but he increasingly wanted to build more products rather than primarily advise on them.

That desire to build became the foundation of his transition toward product management and AI product management.

## Core career progression

Vineet's career can be understood as:

Analytics → Data Science → Product Management → AI Product Management

The common thread is understanding people and data, identifying problems, and building useful solutions.
